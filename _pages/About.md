I am a researcher in Experimental High-Energy Physics at the Faculty of Sciences,
Mohammed University, Rabat, Morocco, and a member of the ATLAS Collaboration at CERN. 
My research focuses on precision measurements of light-by-light scattering in ultra-peripheral
heavy-ion collisions and searches for physics beyond the Standard Model using photon-induced
interactions. My doctoral research centers on the measurement of light-by-light (γγ→γγ) scattering
using the full 2023 Run-3 ATLAS heavy-ion dataset. I have contributed to all stages of the
analysis, including photon reconstruction and identification, event selection, data-driven
background estimation, Monte Carlo production and validation, systematic uncertainty evaluation,
and statistical interpretation. I currently serve as the primary analysis contact for this
measurement, coordinating an international team of researchers through software development,
analysis validation, and the ATLAS internal review process. 

Beyond collider data analysis, I contributed to the High-Granularity Timing
Detector (HGTD) upgrade for the High-Luminosity LHC by designing and implementing the centralized
production database and web interface used to track detector module production and quality
assurance across multiple international sites. I have also contributed to ATLAS detector operations
by taking shifts and supporting the operation and monitoring of the detector. My research interests
also include phenomenological studies of Beyond-the-Standard-Model physics
