    Measurement of Light-by-Light Scattering and Searches for Axion-Like
    Particles and Massive Gravitons in Ultra-Peripheral Heavy-Ion
    Collisions with ATLAS Run 3 Data
  </strong>
</p>

<ul>
  <li>
    <strong>Leadership and Collaboration:</strong> Main analysis contact and lead analyzer. Chair
    analysis meetings and lead the preparation of results for conference presentations and
    journal publications.
  </li>

  <li>
    <strong>Physics strategy:</strong> Proposed a new interpretation strategy
    for searches for massive gravitons.
  </li>

  <li>
    <strong>Monte Carlo production:</strong> Prepared and produced the Monte
    Carlo samples required for the analysis.
  </li>

  <li>
    <strong>Analysis development:</strong> Directed the development of the
    analysis software and contributed to detector calibration and performance
    studies.
  </li>

  <li>
    <strong>Statistical analysis:</strong> Designed and implemented the
    statistical framework used to extract limits on ALPs and massive gravitons.
  </li>

    <p>
    <strong>Production Database for the High-Granularity Timing Detector
      for ATLAS HL-LHC</strong>
    </p>

    <p>
      This project, carried out as my Qualification Authorship Project within
      the ATLAS Collaboration, involved developing the initial MySQL production
      database and web-based tools for managing High-Granularity Timing
      Detector (HGTD) component data.
    </p>

    <p>
      The infrastructure provides detector production tracking, quality-control
      tools, and centralized data management for HGTD production activities
      within the ATLAS Collaboration.
    </p>
