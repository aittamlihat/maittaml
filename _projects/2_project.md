    <h2>Study of Magnetic Monopoles with the KM3NeT-ORCA Neutrino Telescope</h2>

    <p>
      The KM3NeT Collaboration is developing a large-scale Cherenkov neutrino
      telescope deep underwater in the Mediterranean Sea. The ORCA
      (Oscillation Research with Cosmics in the Abyss) detector is designed
      for the study of neutrino oscillations using atmospheric neutrinos.
    </p>

    <p>
      This project focused on two complementary studies. First, a Monte Carlo
      simulation chain for atmospheric muons was developed using the ORCA
      detector geometry, with the aim of testing and validating the simulation
      framework for neutrino-telescope studies.
    </p>

    <p>
      Second, a new reconstruction strategy was investigated for the search for
      an exotic particle, the magnetic monopole. The results obtained with this
      reconstruction method were compared with those from the Monte Carlo
      simulation chain.
    </p>
  </div>
