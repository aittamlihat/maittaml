    <p>
      My Bachelor's final-year project focused on the performance analysis of a
      photovoltaic installation installed at the Faculty of Polydisciplinary
      Studies of Béni Mellal, Sultan Moulay Slimane University.
    </p>

    <p>
      The project evaluated the performance of the PV system using measured
      operational data and the European standard IEC 61724. Monthly power
      production and system behavior under sunny and rainy conditions were
      analyzed. The results showed that power production follows solar
      irradiance and is influenced by environmental conditions, particularly
      irradiance and temperature. The performance ratio was also evaluated as
      an indicator of overall system performance and system losses.
    </p>
